About the Problem

Common digital threats students face every day

01

Phishing

Fake messages pretend to be from schools, banks, or social media to steal passwords and personal information.

02

Online Scams

Scam messages often promise prizes or urgent help, pushing students to click quickly before thinking.

03

Weak Passwords

Simple or reused passwords are easy to guess and can put school, personal, and social accounts at risk.

04

Privacy Risks

Sharing too much information online can expose names, locations, routines, and private details to strangers.

    Cybersecurity Glossary

    Quick meaning of common terms

    Threat

    Phishing

    A scam that tricks people into revealing passwords, codes, or personal data through fake messages or websites.

    Protection

    Two-Factor Authentication

    An extra security step that verifies a login using a second method, such as a code sent to a phone.

    Privacy

    Malware

    Malicious software designed to damage devices, steal data, or take control of systems without permission.

    Risk

    Social Engineering

    Manipulating people into giving away sensitive information by exploiting trust, urgency, or fear.

    Security

    Firewall

    A security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic to block threats.

    Safe practice

    Strong Password

    A long password made from unrelated words, numbers, and symbols that is difficult to guess or crack.

    Cybersecurity Tips

    Simple actions that strengthen your digital safety

    Students can protect themselves by creating strong passwords, enabling extra security, and checking messages carefully before clicking links or downloading files.

    1Create strong passwords using long phrases and avoid reusing them across accounts.
    2Turn on two-factor authentication to add another layer of protection to your accounts.
    3Check sender names, email addresses, and URLs before opening suspicious messages or links.
    4Use trusted apps and updates, and avoid downloading files from unverified sources.
    5Review privacy settings and think carefully before sharing personal information online.
